Monklap is a wall-mounted or desktop diffuser shaped like a friendly monkey. Walk up and clap, and it responds with a pulse of fragrant mist from its mouth. No buttons, no app, nothing to touch.

An ultrasonic sensor checks that someone is near, a sound sensor listens for the clap, and an ESP32 picks a face on the OLED display and fires a 1.5-second burst from a wick-fed ultrasonic atomizer. Designed in Rhino, printed on a Bambu Lab A1, and refined over four rounds of printing and fitting.
