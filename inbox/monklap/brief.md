Monklap is a wall-mounted or desktop diffuser shaped like a friendly monkey. Walk up and clap, and it responds with a pulse of fragrant mist from its mouth.

No buttons, no app, nothing to touch.

## Scent, on cue | side
Monklap is for people who want scent to feel like a moment of play, not another chore.

Walk up and clap, and it responds with a pulse of fragrant mist from its mouth. Touchless, interactive, ambient.

Three groups: aesthetic and tech enthusiasts, touchless and functional users, and experiential spaces such as pop-ups, cafés and galleries.

## How it works | side
Two sensors read the room, an ESP32 picks a face, and the atomizer answers with a 1.5-second burst of mist.

Everything lives inside the face: the OLED eyes, ultrasonic sensors in the nostrils, the atomizer in the mouth.

Fill the tank, twist the bayonet lid into the head, hang it on a single screw and clap.

## From a button to a clap | side
Monklap began as a "Sound & Scent System" for public spaces. The button became a clap, and the form became a monkey.

Four rounds of modeling, printing, soldering and fitting took it from sketches to a working prototype.
